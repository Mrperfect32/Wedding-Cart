PREMIUM ONLINE WEDDING INVITATION — V3

1. Open index.html in VS Code / Live Server.
2. Click OPEN OUR INVITATION to enjoy the two-sided envelope, glow and butterflies.
3. Gallery photos are demo images from Unsplash (internet connection required). Replace each img src AND data-full in index.html with your own photo paths.
4. For the Wedding Film section: add your own MP4 file at assets/wedding-film.mp4. Without a file, the video player will show an instruction message.
5. Change names, dates, address and venue in index.html, and the countdown date in script.js. Map URLs are demo links; replace them with the correct venue map URL.
6. There is NO RSVP, attendance form or guest count. This is an invitation card only.
7. This website runs without any backend. For online hosting, upload this entire folder and its assets. Google Fonts, sample gallery and film poster require internet.
